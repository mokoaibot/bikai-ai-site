TQ 1621

Triple Quadrupole LC-MS/MS · Models A and B

View original source page ↗

BIKAI TQ 1621 triple quadrupole LC-MS/MS achieves femtogram-level instrument detection limit, operating purely with nitrogen gas without requiring an argon gas cylinder. Equipped with a 180° curved collision cell with axial linear acceleration, it eliminates neutral particles from the ion beam and restricts MRM crosstalk down to 0.01%, preserving excellent sensitivity even for complex real-world sample matrices. The complete system is delivered with an integrated PSA nitrogen generator and oil-free dry fore pump with a 5-year maintenance-free service life, removing the hassle of argon supply contracts, gas cylinder management and regular oil changes.
