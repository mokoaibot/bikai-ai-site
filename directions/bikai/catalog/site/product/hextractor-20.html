Automated Sample Preparation System hExtractor 20

Extraction · Hydrolysis · Digestion · Oxidation · Organic Synthesis

Officially named Auto-Chemical Extractor / Auto-Chemical Reactor (ACR20), offered in two models: sequential hExtractor 20 and batch-type hExtractor 20B. An integrated, fully automatic pretreatment instrument with high-pressure heating and reflux functions that replaces traditional manual lab pretreatment equipment (Soxhlet extractors, ultrasonic cleaners, microwave processors, high-pressure digesters, reflux hydrolysis devices) — realizing full automation of chemical laboratory sample prep.

Applications: rapid extraction of plastic additives (antioxidants, flame retardants, stabilizers) from PP/rubber/PET/nylon in minutes instead of hours; PET/nylon depolymerization for monomer analysis; high-pressure nitric-acid digestion of plastics for heavy-metal testing; PFAS Total Oxidisable Precursor (TOP) assay (5–20 min vs. 12 h traditionally, >99.9% oxidation efficiency); extraction of dioxins/PBDEs from soil and waste; extraction of human metabolites from hair & nails (nicotine, cannabinoids, fatty acids) with up to 100% recovery; herbal-medicine active-ingredient extraction (e.g. ginsenosides); rapid protein hydrolysis for amino-acid testing (5–30 min vs. 12–24 h traditionally); polysaccharide hydrolysis; and general high-pressure chemical synthesis/crystallization reactions (0.5–2 h vs. 4–120 h traditionally).

Key advantages: dramatic time savings (e.g. Soxhlet extraction 1–72 h → 2–10 min on the hExtractor); micro-solvent consumption (0.1–5 mL vs. 50–200 mL, cutting organic solvent use by over 99%); full-process automation (automatic sample feeding → closed high-pressure reaction/heated reflux → cooling & depressurization → automatic dilution, no manual operation); touchscreen control storing up to 20 methods (temperature 50–300°C, heating duration 0–16 h); and a multi-layer safety protection system with graded pressure-resistant reaction sleeves for risk-free high-pressure operation.
